Founded: 1987 | Headquarters: Austin, Texas

ABOUT US

Caro Car Company (commonly known as CCC or 3C) is a fictional automotive retailer created for educational and RAG testing purposes. In this test dataset, CCC is portrayed as a mid-size American car dealership group specializing in new vehicles, certified pre-owned cars, electric vehicles, and full-service maintenance.

HISTORY

1987 — Eleanor Caro opens the first Caro Car showroom on Motorway Drive in Austin, Texas, selling 12 vehicles in the first month.

1995 — CCC expands to Dallas and Houston; introduces the "3C" shorthand logo.

2003 — Launches Caro Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) program with 150-point inspection standard.

2012 — Opens first out-of-state location in Miami, Florida.

2018 — Debuts electric vehicle division with the Caro Volt concept.

2022 — Reaches 8 showroom locations across 6 states; 50,000+ vehicles sold lifetime (fictional milestone).

2026 — Piloting CCC DRIVE vehicle subscription and Seattle expansion.
