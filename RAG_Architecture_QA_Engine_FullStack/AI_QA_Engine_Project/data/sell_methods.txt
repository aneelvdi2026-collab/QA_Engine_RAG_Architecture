CARO CAR COMPANY (CCC / 3C) — HOW TO BUY A VEHICLE
Sales methods and purchase options

Caro Car Company offers multiple ways to purchase or acquire a vehicle. Choose the method that fits your needs.

--- 1. IN-STORE PURCHASE (TRADITIONAL) ---

Visit any CCC showroom to work with a sales consultant.
Steps:
  a. Browse showroom floor and lot inventory
  b. Schedule a test drive (30 minutes, license required)
  c. Receive a written quote including taxes, fees, and add-ons
  d. Choose payment method: cash, finance, or lease
  e. Sign purchase agreement and pay deposit ($500 for reservations)
  f. Take delivery in 1–5 business days (in-stock vehicles)

Best for: Buyers who want hands-on experience and immediate answers.

--- 2. ONLINE PURCHASE ---

Available at www.carocar.example/shop
Steps:
  a. Search inventory across all 3C locations
  b. Configure payment: finance pre-qualification or cash
  c. Add trade-in estimate (final value confirmed in person)
  d. Pay refundable $500 reservation fee
  e. Choose delivery: home delivery ($199–$499 by distance) or pickup at nearest CCC store
  f. Complete paperwork electronically; sign at delivery

Online purchase discount: $250 off documentation fee for fully digital transactions.

--- 3. FINANCING ---

Partner: Caro Finance Group
Options:
  - New vehicle loans: 2.9%–7.9% APR for 36–72 months (credit dependent)
  - Used/CPO loans: 4.5%–9.9% APR for 36–60 months
  - Pre-qualification: soft credit check, no impact on score
  - Same-day approval available at all Texas showrooms

Minimum down payment: $0 on approved credit for select promotions; typical 10% recommended.

--- 4. LEASING ---

Available on new CCC models only.
Terms: 24, 36, or 48 months
Mileage options: 10,000 / 12,000 / 15,000 miles per year
Example: Caro Cross CX-5 — $299/month for 36 months, $2,500 due at signing (July 2026 promo)
Excess mileage: $0.20/mile
Lease-end: return, buy out, or lease a new 3C model

--- 5. TRADE-IN ---

CCC accepts trade-ins toward any purchase method.
Process:
  a. Enter vehicle details online or in-store for instant estimate
  b. In-person inspection required within 7 days of quote
  c. Trade-in credit applied to down payment or purchase price
  d. CCC handles payoff of existing liens (up to 10 business days)

Not accepted: salvage titles, flood damage, vehicles older than 2015 (exceptions for classics at manager discretion).

--- 6. FLEET & CORPORATE SALES ---

Contact fleet@carocar.example for businesses buying 5+ vehicles.
Benefits: volume pricing (3–8% off MSRP), dedicated account manager, custom billing
Minimum order: 5 vehicles
Delivery: staged delivery to multiple CCC locations or single corporate address

--- 7. SUBSCRIPTION (CCC DRIVE) ---

Monthly vehicle subscription — pilot program in Austin and Dallas only.
Price: $599–$1,299/month depending on model tier
Includes: insurance, maintenance, roadside assistance
Swap vehicle: once per 90 days
Contract: 3-month minimum, month-to-month after
Models available: Caro Cross CX-5, Caro Volt S, Caro Civic Line CL-200

--- 8. AUCTION / OUTLET ---

CCC Outlet Center — San Antonio Central (Store #004)
Sells: previous rental fleet, demo vehicles, minor cosmetic damage units
Discount: 10–25% below standard used pricing
Sales method: fixed price (no bidding); first-come, first-served
Warranty: 90-day/3,000-mile limited powertrain on outlet vehicles
