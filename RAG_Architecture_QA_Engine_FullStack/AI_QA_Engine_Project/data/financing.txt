CARO CAR COMPANY (CCC / 3C) — FINANCING & PAYMENT OPTIONS
Partner: Caro Finance Group | Updated: 2026

OVERVIEW

Caro Car Company works with Caro Finance Group and National Auto Credit to offer loans, leases, and flexible payment plans. Pre-qualification is available online with a soft credit inquiry.

--- AUTO LOANS (PURCHASE) ---

New Vehicles
  APR range: 2.9% – 7.9%
  Terms: 36, 48, 60, 72, or 84 months
  Best rate (2.9%): Requires 750+ credit score, 20% down, 60-month term on select models
  Maximum loan amount: $150,000

Certified Pre-Owned (CPO)
  APR range: 4.5% – 9.9%
  Terms: 36, 48, or 60 months
  Vehicles: Model years 2022–2025, under 60,000 miles

Used (Non-CPO)
  APR range: 6.9% – 12.9%
  Terms: 36 or 48 months
  Vehicles: 2015 or newer at CCC outlets

--- LEASE OPTIONS ---

Provider: Caro Finance Group Lease Division
Terms: 24, 36, or 48 months
Annual mileage: 10,000 | 12,000 | 15,000
Excess mileage fee: $0.20/mile
Disposition fee at lease end: $395 (waived if leasing another 3C vehicle)
Money factor equivalent: 0.00125 – 0.00350 (credit dependent)

--- DOWN PAYMENT ---

Minimum: $0 on approved credit during promotional periods
Recommended: 10–20% of purchase price
Trade-in equity counts toward down payment
CCC accepts: cash, cashier's check, wire transfer, and certified funds
