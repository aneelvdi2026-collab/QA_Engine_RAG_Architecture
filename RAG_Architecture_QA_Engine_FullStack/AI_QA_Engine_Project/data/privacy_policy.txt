CARO CAR COMPANY (CCC / 3C) — PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: January 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Caro Car Company ("CCC", "3C", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit our showrooms, use our website at www.carocar.example, or purchase a vehicle from us.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We may collect:
- Full name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
- Driver's license number and date of birth (for test drives and financing)
- Payment information (processed by third-party providers; CCC does not store full credit card numbers)
- Vehicle preferences, trade-in details, and service history
- Website usage data, cookies, and IP address

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use collected data to:
- Process vehicle purchases, leases, and trade-ins
- Arrange test drives and deliver vehicles
- Send promotional offers about CCC models and seasonal sales (you may opt out)
- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
- Improve our customer service and website experience

3. DATA SHARING

CCC does not sell your personal data. We may share information with:
- Financing partners (Caro Finance Group, National Auto Credit)
- Insurance referral partners
- Service centers affiliated with 3C
- Law enforcement when required by law

4. DATA RETENTION

Customer records are retained for 7 years after the last transaction. Marketing consent records are kept until you unsubscribe.

5. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by emailing privacy@carocar.example or calling 1-800-CCC-AUTO (1-800-222-2886).

6. SECURITY

We use encryption for online forms and restrict employee access to customer databases. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. CHILDREN

CCC services are not directed at individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Contact: Privacy Officer, Caro Car Company, 100 Motorway Drive, Austin, TX 78701
