CARO CAR COMPANY (CCC / 3C) — WARRANTY & SERVICE
Service brand: CCC Service Centers

--- NEW VEHICLE WARRANTIES (MANUFACTURER) ---

All new Caro vehicles include factory coverage:

Basic (Bumper-to-Bumper)
  Duration: 3 years or 36,000 miles, whichever comes first
  Covers: Most vehicle components except wear items (tires, brake pads, wiper blades)

Powertrain
  Duration: 5 years or 60,000 miles
  Covers: Engine, transmission, drive axles, seals and gaskets

EV Battery (Electric Models Only)
  Duration: 8 years or 100,000 miles
  Covers: Battery capacity retention — replacement if capacity falls below 70%
  Applies to: Caro Volt S, Caro Eco Cross, Caro Spark Mini EV

Corrosion
  Duration: 5 years unlimited miles
  Covers: Perforation from rust-through on body panels

Roadside Assistance
  Duration: 5 years or 60,000 miles
  Includes: Towing (up to 50 miles), jump start, flat tire change, lockout service, fuel delivery (2 gallons)

--- CERTIFIED PRE-OWNED (CPO) WARRANTY ---

CCC adds limited coverage on top of any remaining factory warranty:

3C CPO Limited Warranty
  Duration: 12 months or 12,000 miles from purchase date
  Covers: Engine, transmission, electrical, A/C, fuel system
  Deductible: $100 per visit

CPO Roadside Assistance
  Duration: 12 months from purchase
  Same benefits as new vehicle roadside program

150-Point Inspection
  Every CPO vehicle passes inspection covering mechanical, cosmetic, and history checks.
  Vehicle history report provided free (Caro Vehicle History).

--- OUTLET VEHICLES ---

CCC Outlet (demo, rental fleet, cosmetic damage units):
  90-day or 3,000-mile limited powertrain warranty
  No bumper-to-bumper coverage
  Sold as-is on cosmetic notes — disclosed at sale

--- ROUTINE MAINTENANCE ---

Recommended intervals (gas models):
  Oil change: Every 5,000 miles or 6 months
  Tire rotation: Every 7,500 miles
  Brake inspection: Every 15,000 miles
  Transmission fluid: Every 60,000 miles

EV maintenance:
  Cabin air filter: Every 15,000 miles
  Brake fluid: Every 3 years
  Battery coolant: Inspect at 100,000 miles
